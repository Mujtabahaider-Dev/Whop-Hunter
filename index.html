How to Add to Your Browser

Takes under 30 seconds. Works seamlessly across any Chromium-based browser.

1

Download Extension Folder

Download the repository as a ZIP from GitHub and extract the folder onto your computer.

Whop-Hunter.zip Download
2

Open Extensions Manager

Navigate to chrome://extensions and switch on the Developer mode toggle in the top-right corner.

chrome://extensions Developer mode: ON
3

Click "Load unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left, select the extracted folder, and Whop Hunter will instantly appear in your extensions!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Whop Hunter require an account?

No. Whop Hunter is completely barrier-free. There is no sign-up, email collection, or authentication required.

Is there a limit on how many times I can filter?

No. Unlike proprietary tools that limit free users to 5 filter runs per month, Whop Hunter allows unlimited filter runs forever.

How does Whop Hunter retrieve platform-specific rates?

When you browse Whop's Discover listing, the extension taps into the client-side network stream and card endpoints to read the full payout payload, decoding individual platform rates without navigating away.

Does this tool collect or transmit my browsing data?

No. The extension runs locally within your browser context. It does not send analytics, telemetry, or campaign data to external servers.

Is Whop Hunter officially affiliated with Whop?

No. Whop Hunter is an open-source community utility and is not affiliated with, maintained by, or endorsed by Whop.com.
