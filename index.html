The real numbers

One rate on the listing. A different one per platform.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Whop Hunter requires zero login or sign-up. Just install the unpacked extension and it works immediately.

What counts as a filter run?

Each time you tune your sliders or filters and click Filter, that's a run. Unlike paid tools that limit you to 5 runs, you get unlimited runs for free.

What can I filter by?

Price per 1k views per platform, budget total, percentage already paid out, views remaining, clipper count, campaign age, and owner verification.

Where does it work?

Directly on the official Whop Content Rewards discovery page (whop.com/discover/app/app_QRxsQodZgK1r4D).

Does it send my browsing anywhere?

Never. Whop Hunter runs 100% client-side inside your own browser. No data, telemetry, or browsing history is ever transmitted.

Is Whop Hunter made by Whop?

No, Whop Hunter is an independent open-source tool built by creators, for creators. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Whop.

Which browsers does it support?

Any Chromium-based browser: Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Arc, and Opera.
